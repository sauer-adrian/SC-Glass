Connect to your PC

This is a demo. Controls light up and show what they would press, but nothing reaches a game. The ship and session panels show example data.

  1. On the gaming PC, run SCGlass.exe or start.bat.
  2. On this tablet, scan the QR code it shows, or open the address it prints, on the same Wi-Fi.
  3. Enter the 6-digit PIN once. Keep Star Citizen as the active window while you play.
